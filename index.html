About the academy

Football, done properly

Simolat Football Academy describes itself as a player-centred institution in Nigeria. Its stated focus is identifying and nurturing young football talent through structured coaching, competitive trials and training tours.

The academy says its system monitors and supports players' educational development, and that it exposes players to professional trials at home and abroad. Its published objectives include player wellbeing, skill development, discipline, fair play and sportsmanship.

Registration information

Before you enquire

The following requirements and terms are published on the academy website. Confirm current requirements and payment terms directly with the academy before registering.

Published age groups

Junior players: 5–15 years. Senior players: 16–21 years. The website says players under 18 must attend tryouts with a parent or guardian.

Documents listed

Medical history and physical clearance (the site mentions ECG/PCMA tests); birth certificate; NIN; two player passport photographs; and school results (primary/secondary or WAEC/NECO). Under 18 players also need a parent/guardian photograph and must attend tryouts with a parent.

Registration options

The site describes academic-standard registration using a player profile, guardian consent, medical history and school results; a scholarship route with educational development; and a professional-contract employment offer said to include wages and club registration. Confirm availability, eligibility and full terms directly with the academy.

The official website states that registration, tuition and admission payments are non-refundable and describes them as covering onboarding, medical insurance and support, slot reservation, kit, development programmes and player-profile processing. This policy and the listed inclusions need confirmation from the academy before publication as final terms.
